How to play

Defend your base through three levels and nine waves.

Protect the base

Choose your name, difficulty and map: Meadow, Desert or Tundra. You start with 20 base lives. Each enemy that reaches the end of the road takes one life. Clear all nine waves to win.

Build your defense

Select a tower in the shop, then click empty ground to place it. Leave room between towers and keep them off the road. You can build during a wave too.

Upgrade and merge

Click a placed tower and choose its damage, speed or range path. Upgrades cost 35 coins for level 2 and 65 for level 3. The chosen path stays with that tower.

Merge two level 3 towers of the same type and path to make one level 4 tower. Selling returns half of the tower's purchase and upgrade costs.

Take a chance

After waves 1, 4 and 7 on Easy or Normal, choose one of three chance cards. On Hard, cards appear after waves 1 and 6. Effects stack and last for the rest of the run, including for new towers.

Earn your score

Each defeated enemy earns 10 points and 10 coins. Clearing a wave adds 50 points and 30 coins; completing a level adds another 100 points and 40 coins.

At the end of each run, the game sends its score to the local server. The result screen confirms whether it saved. The leaderboard shows the best 30 completed runs on this computer.

Pick up where you left off

Your unfinished run saves automatically. Use Continue saved run from the menu to return to it, then resume the paused game.

Auto wave starts the next wave after a three-second wait when you have a tower. Choose any pending chance card before continuing.
